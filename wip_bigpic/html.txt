      <p class="bt-note">Large speculators (non-commercial traders) in the CFTC legacy futures-only report. Net = long minus short contracts. Index: where today's net position sits between its low (0) and high (100) of the last 1, 3 and 5 years of weekly reports. Signal uses the 3-year index: 80 or above bullish, 20 or below bearish. Trend: the 3-year index over the last 26 weeks. Positions are as of Tuesday and published the following Friday. Extreme readings show crowding; they have often come near turning points, so read them as positioning, not as a forecast.</p>

    <p class="bt-note">Layout modelled on WallStreetCourier's Big Picture page. Health scores, regimes and positioning indices here are our own, built from daily closes (Yahoo Finance) and the CFTC public reporting API; their ~40-indicator scores and Smart Money Flow Index are proprietary and not reproduced. Health per horizon averages 5&ndash;7 price-only inputs: price vs moving averages, moving-average slopes, returns, the fit and up-day share of the trend, RSI and position in the trading range. Trend inputs are scaled by each market's own volatility so that all 29 markets score on one scale. No constituent breadth data is used. CSI 300 uses the ASHR ETF and Emerging Markets the EEM ETF.</p>
